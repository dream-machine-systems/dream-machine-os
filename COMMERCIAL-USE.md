## What "commercial" means

This repository uses the definition of the license itself. NonCommercial means "not primarily intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation" (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, Section 1(k)).

This page explains how Dream Machine Systems FlexCo applies that definition. It is guidance, not an additional condition. Where this page and the license differ, the license wins.

## Free (no request needed)

- Applying the protocols in your own life, relationships and leadership.
- Study groups, peer circles and non-profit associations, also when they charge a fee that only covers costs.
- Teaching at schools and universities.
- Quoting, reviewing, critiquing or explaining the spec, also on monetized channels such as a blog, podcast or video.
- Translating or adapting the spec for non-commercial publication, with attribution and under the same license.

## Enterprise license required

- Paid coaching, counselling or therapy sessions, workshops or seminars that use material from this repository.
- Corporate training, team programs or internal enablement delivered for or inside a company or public authority.
- Products, apps, courses, certifications or translations sold on the basis of this material.
- Using the names **Dream Machine®** or **Love Protocol®** to market an offering, see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

Copyright protects the text, not the ideas. Applying the method in conversation, without copying material and without using the names, needs no license.
