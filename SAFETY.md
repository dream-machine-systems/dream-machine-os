# Safety Boundary

The Dream Machine OS is an architectural framework for self-observation. It is not psychotherapy, medical treatment or crisis support.

## If you are in crisis

Stop reading. Contact a person now.

- Emergency (EU): **112**
- Austria, Telefonseelsorge: **142** (24/7, free)
- Germany, TelefonSeelsorge: **0800 111 0 111**
- Other countries: [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com)

## Do not post personal data here

Issues, pull requests and comments in this repository are public and effectively permanent. Do not post health information, relationship details, trauma narratives or anything that identifies you or a third party. Field reports go to [dreammachine.systems/telemetry](https://www.dreammachine.systems/telemetry), which is private.

## Reporting a safety concern in the spec

If a module could cause harm when executed as written (dissociation, suppression of symptoms, unsafe physical practice), do not open a public issue. Report it privately via [dreammachine.systems/feedback](https://www.dreammachine.systems/feedback) with the category **Safety concern**. Safety reports take priority over every other change.
