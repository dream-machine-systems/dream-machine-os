A severe, autonomic hard shutdown initiated by the biological hardware when overwhelmed by a critical system shock or unresolvable network partition. It acts as a protective physiological freeze state designed to prevent permanent motherboard damage when the local processor lacks the bandwidth to handle a massive analog payload.
