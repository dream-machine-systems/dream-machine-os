A resonance error where the node mirrors a failing peer's raw pain matrix instead of routing support to it—feeling with the node rather than for it. Left unpatched, it escalates to a Sev-1 Buffer Overflow (Empathic Distress) and crashes the would-be helper. The Compassion Patch (Karuna) is the corrective.
