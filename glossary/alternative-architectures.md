Non-standard biological processor designs (e.g., ADHD, Autism). An alternative architecture is not a degraded or "broken" x86 chip; it is an entirely different architecture (like an ARM processor) that requires unique instruction sets, custom environments, and different scheduler defaults to run optimally.
