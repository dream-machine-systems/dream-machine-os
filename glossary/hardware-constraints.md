Fixed, un-patchable physical limitations of a node's chassis (e.g., chronic illness, physical disability, or reduced biological capacity). Nodes operating under these constraints are Sudo Admins by necessity, as they must proactively manage a strict power envelope (TDP) and energy allocation with zero margin for error.
