The highly stateful, biologically required network protocol for mammalian safety. Unlike a stateless UDP Broadcast, TCP requires a constant, verifiable SYN/ACK response (co-regulation and validation) from the target node to feel secure. It inherently generates system friction and high latency, but guarantees deep packet delivery.
