Active mindfulness. The raw, unfiltered monitoring of inbound data packets (sensations) in real-time before the intellect attaches a narrative to them. The term is also used for other monitors that detect a signal before any response, for example empathy (Chapter 9.4.1) and sleep-tracking peripherals (Chapter 5.4.2).
