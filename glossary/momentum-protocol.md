A workaround for the Procrastination Bug. Bypassing the motivation GUI (which requires the system to "feel" ready) to force a low-overhead hardware execution trigger. By initiating micro-movements, the system rapidly generates the kinetic energy and dopamine required to sustain the task, breaking the infinite wait loop.
