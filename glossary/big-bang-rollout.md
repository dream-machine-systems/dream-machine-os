A critical deployment error where a node attempts a 100% architectural rewrite of their behaviors, habits, and trauma overnight. Because the biological motherboard cannot process massive neuroplastic updates simultaneously, the system rapidly thermal-throttles and reverts to the corrupted legacy baseline.
