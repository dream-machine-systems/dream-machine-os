The centralized diagnostic interface used to monitor system health at a glance. In a biological crisis, it replaces chaotic emotional overwhelm with strict, deterministic routing, allowing the Sudo Admin to instantly isolate whether the crash is occurring at the BIOS, Processor, or Network layer.
