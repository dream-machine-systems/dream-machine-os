The target ~500-millisecond gap forced between a malicious input (stimulus) and the chosen output (response). The space where the autonomic reflex is actively killed and choice exists. Crucially, it functions as an active mental rate limiter against algorithmic hyper-speed, preventing the system from being hijacked by high-salience botnets and outrage loops.
