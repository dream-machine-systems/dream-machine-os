The biological equivalent of working memory. Unlike silicon RAM, which relies on a stable, external electrical grid, Cognitive RAM draws power directly from your internal cellular Power Supply Unit (glucose and ATP). It is a volatile, physically limited resource. Executing rapid context switching (multitasking) or running unoptimized background processes (such as rumination or anxiety) physically drains this memory. When Cognitive RAM is depleted, the system experiences dropped threads, cognitive fatigue, and localized CPU Thermal Throttling.
