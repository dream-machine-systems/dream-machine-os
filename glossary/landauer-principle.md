The thermodynamic law stating that deleting information from a physical storage medium inevitably produces physical heat. In this architecture, it serves as the core heuristic for autonomic release: when a node experiences shaking, crying, or sweating during Shadow Work, it is not emotional instability. It is modeled as the physical heat dissipation required to mechanically discharge obsolete somatic tension from the chassis.
