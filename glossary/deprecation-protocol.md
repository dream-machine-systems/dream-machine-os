The correct method for retiring a maladaptive emotional response loop. Rather than attempting a forced `rm -rf` (repression, which violates Immutable Event Sourcing), the script is quarantined in a Sandbox and gradually starved of execution priority until it deprecates naturally. You do not delete the legacy code; you stop calling it.
