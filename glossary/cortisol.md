The biological chemical deployed to forcefully Overclock the physical hardware during an emergency. It spikes blood glucose to temporarily maximize CPU output. If this chemical is not physically metabolized and vented from the chassis (via heavy exertion or somatic release), it acts as Somatic Technical Debt, permanently raising the ambient case temperature until the motherboard triggers a Thermal Throttle (Burnout).
