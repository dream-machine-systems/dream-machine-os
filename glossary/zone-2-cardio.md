Low-intensity sustained conditioning whose assigned system role is expanding the total size of the cellular Power Supply. The structural counterpart to System Overclocking (HIIT): HIIT vents accumulated cortisol in short bursts, whereas Zone 2 slowly upgrades the underlying battery hardware itself.
