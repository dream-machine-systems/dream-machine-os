A severe influx of data completely overwhelming the hardware's processing bandwidth at the time of impact. Unable to safely compile the input, the OS forcibly quarantines the raw, unexpressed kinetic energy in the physical chassis (fascial cache). Until manually discharged via Root Access, it runs as a permanent, highly volatile background loop draining system battery.
