A severe biological energy deficit mistakenly diagnosed as a cognitive software flaw. A state where cellular Power Supply Units lack the ATP required to run high-level executive logic, forcing the prefrontal cortex offline and locking the system into a limbic survival loop. Resolution requires physical refueling, not psychological refactoring.
