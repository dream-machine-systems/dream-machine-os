A toxic collective network that recruits exhausted nodes. It promises stability, but secretly revokes Admin rights and hijacks the local CPU to spread Fragmentation Malware. Modern botnets (e.g., radical populism, the digital "Manosphere") specifically target economically alienated nodes, weaponizing their un-metabolized grief into algorithmic rage.
